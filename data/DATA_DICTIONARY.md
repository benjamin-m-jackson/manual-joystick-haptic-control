# Data dictionary

All files are UTF-8 CSV with a header row. An empty cell means "not available". They are generated by `analysis/build_deidentified_data.py` (private) from the study's raw records. **Do not edit them by hand.**

**Participants.** `P01`–`P09` are random codes. The key linking codes to identities is held by the study team and is not released. The tenth recruited participant, an expert, completed the experiment only partially and is not included (see `missing_and_excluded.csv`).

**Expertise.** `Novice` means ≤5 years and `Expert` means >5 years of self-reported interventional neuroradiology experience. Years of experience are not released.

**Controller labels.** `Manual` (manual navigation), `Haptic On` and `Haptic Off` (the device-mimicking robotic controller with haptic feedback on and off), and `Joystick`. `repeat` is 1 for the initial attempt and 2 for the repeat.

## `navigation_trials.csv`

One row per performed navigation: 69 rows (9 participants × 8 planned, less 3 not performed).

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `participant`, `expertise`, `controller`, `repeat` | As above. |
| `block_order` | `manual first` or `robotic first`: whether the manual block (both manual attempts) came before or after the robotic block. Empty where it could not be established. |
| `trial_position` | Position of this navigation in the participant's session, from 1 to the number of navigations performed. Empty where the full order could not be established. |
| `robotic_position` | Position within the robotic block (1–6). Empty for manual trials and where the order could not be established. |
| `t_BCA_s`, `t_CCA_s`, `t_ICA_s` | Cumulative time in seconds from the start of the navigation to reaching the brachiocephalic artery, the right common carotid artery and the right internal carotid artery (the target). `480` means the eight-minute limit was reached before that checkpoint, and the navigation was stopped. |
| `reached_ICA` | 1 if the target was reached within the limit (`t_ICA_s` < 480), otherwise 0. |
| `incorrect_catheterizations` | Number of incorrect catheterizations: microcatheter or guidewire insertion of more than 10 mm into a vessel not part of the planned route. In stopped navigations, counted up to the point of termination. |
| `prolapse_events` | Number of prolapse events: the microcatheter or guidewire buckling or slipping backward out of the origin of the target branch vessel by more than 10 mm. Counted in the same way. |
| `force_data` | 1 if a force log is available for this navigation (`force_peak_per_sensor.csv`), otherwise 0. |
| `force_log_s` | Duration of the navigation's force log, in seconds. The log starts with the navigation. Some logs continue after the target was reached. |
| `force_log_covers_navigation` | 1 if the force log lasts at least `t_ICA_s` − 30 s. 0 for three navigations stopped at the eight-minute limit whose log ended early (274–365 s); their peaks cover only part of the navigation. Empty where there is no force log. |

Trial order is taken from the first timestamp of each navigation's force log, cross-checked against the start times of the video records. One participant's manual block has no force log, so its position comes from the study team's record. For one participant, the time of one navigation (which has no force log or video) is unknown, so that participant's order is left empty. That participant's session also departed from the protocol: the manual block was performed between robotic navigations.

## `force_peak_per_sensor.csv`

One row per navigation and sensor: 528 rows (66 navigations with a force log × 8 sensors).

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `participant`, `expertise`, `controller`, `repeat` | As above. |
| `sensor` | Sensor number 1–8, as numbered in the manuscript (Fig. 3). |
| `channel` | Logger channel of that sensor: `ch1`, `ch2`, `ch6`, `ch7`, `ch10`, `ch13`, `ch14`, `ch15` = sensors 1–8. |
| `peak_force_mN` | Maximum force recorded by the sensor over the whole force log, in mN (logged in N and multiplied by 1000). This is the outcome analysed in the manuscript. Values are quantized by the logger. For example, sensor 2 takes values in steps of about 4.8 mN, so identical values in different trials are expected. |
| `peak_force_nav_window_mN` | Maximum force over the first `t_ICA_s` + 5 s of the log only, i.e. excluding any recording after the target was reached. Used for the sensitivity analysis. |

**Correction in revision R2.** For three navigations, the file labelled as the trial's force log did not record the trial:
- **Two were practice runs.** Each started with the practice video and was shorter than the trial. The trial's own log is used instead; its start time matches the trial video to within 2 s.
- **One was a preview run.** The trial's own log is the only log recorded at the trial's place in the session order.

All three replaced files are listed in `missing_and_excluded.csv`. Peak forces for these three navigations therefore differ from those analysed in the R1 version of the manuscript. All other values are unchanged.

## `questionnaire_ratings.csv`

One row per participant: 9 rows. Each column holds the participant's answer to one form item. **`questionnaire_items.csv` gives the exact wording of every item** as it appeared on the form (Supplementary Material S1).

- All rated items use a 1–10 scale. The right-hand anchor is "Strongly Agree" for the agreement items and "Very high" for the NASA-TLX items.
- `realism_*`: perceived realism of the fluoroscopic image and the phantom.
- `intuitive_<controller>`: "the … was intuitive" for each controller.
- `tlx_<controller>_<dimension>`: NASA-TLX raw ratings (mental, physical, temporal, performance, effort, frustration) for each controller.
- `sus_01`–`sus_10`: the ten System Usability Scale statements, in standard order. The SUS score (0–100) reported in the manuscript is 2.5 × [Σ over odd items of (x − 1) × 4/9 + Σ over even items of (10 − x) × 4/9]. This rescales each 1–10 answer to the standard 0–4 item score.
- `training_teaching_tool`, `training_practice_tool`: perceived training utility.
- `most_intuitive_robotic_controller`: the robotic controller the participant found most intuitive (answer text as exported).
- One participant did not complete the manual section, so their `intuitive_manual` and `tlx_manual_*` cells are empty.

## `questionnaire_comments.csv`

The free-text answers: one row per non-empty answer.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `participant` | As above. |
| `question` | The comment item (wording in `questionnaire_items.csv`): `comment_realism`, `comment_manual`, `improve_manual_realism`, `comment_haptic_on`, `comment_haptic_off`, `comment_joystick`. |
| `comment` | The answer, verbatim, except that any personal names are replaced by `[name]`. |

## `questionnaire_items.csv`

For every column above: the file it belongs to, the exact item wording from the form export, and the response type.

## `missing_and_excluded.csv`

Every planned navigation or recording that is absent from an analysis, with the reason. Some sessions also included practice runs and navigations to the left internal carotid artery. These were not part of this study and are not included; only those that appear among the labelled trial logs are listed. Where a navigation was restarted shortly after it began, the completed attempt is the one analysed.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `participant`, `controller`, `repeat` | The navigation concerned (empty for recordings that are not study trials). |
| `missing_from` | `all analyses`, `force analyses only`, or `not a study trial`. |
| `reason` | Why the navigation or recording is missing or excluded. |
